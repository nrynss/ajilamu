## Contract changes

These fixes sit outside the T5.7 `owns` line. T5.7 cannot make them. Each one belongs to the
task named beside it.

1. **T5.3 · `Timeline.svelte` needs a selection input and a playhead.** F8 cannot close inside
   the route. The component accepts only `segments`, `tracks`, and `durationMs`. It needs a
   `selectedSegmentId` prop, an `onselect` callback, and a playback position input.
2. **T5.5 · `Rail.svelte` needs an active language input.** F5 cannot close inside the route.
   The component hardcodes `dub.languages[0]`.
3. **T5.2 · `Preview.svelte` misses `loadedmetadata` during hydration.** On the composed route
   the element reports `readyState` 4 and `duration` 75.008267 while the component still holds
   zero. The component should read `video.duration` once on mount rather than wait for an event
   that already fired.
4. **T5.2 · the fixture carries no per-language mixed track.** F4 needs either a mixed audio
   asset per language or a `Preview` contract that plays discrete takes in sequence.
5. **T5.3 · `Track.svelte` paints a fitting take as an overrun.** Lines 3 and 4 render in the
   `--over` colour because their deltas are plus 18 and plus 2 milliseconds, although both
   carry `state: "fits"`. Two of eight dubbed blocks read as failures on sight.
6. **T5.1 · the chrome hardcodes readiness and spend.** The header reads "Ready for review" and
   "$0.02 spent" regardless of the payload. It kept both strings with `readiness` forced to
   `running` and traces to no charge in the project total of $0.023414.
