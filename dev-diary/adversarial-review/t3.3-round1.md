Every measurement selected `/opt/homebrew/bin/ffmpeg` and `/opt/homebrew/bin/ffprobe`, both version 9.0.1.
`PATH=/opt/homebrew/bin:/usr/bin:/bin`. Go reports version 1.27.1.

### Silent intervals stay with the bed

The synthetic bed is an 80 Hz stereo sine at volume 0.2. Speech is a 2 kHz tone from 1 s to 2 s.

Seconds 0.2 through 0.8 of bed and mix both measure -38.1 dB mean and -35.1 dB maximum.
Those windows share SHA-1 `066b4f92a502b3aa8dc989a6addd76cefb7fafc5`.
Decoded samples differ by 0.000000 dB. The SHA-1 of each window matches.

Seconds 2.5 through 3.5 repeat that exact match after speech ends.
The 2.05 s to 2.25 s release window also matches at 0.000000 dB.

A constant `volume=0.20` plus default `amix` drops the same silent lowpass window to -58.2 dB.
That is 20.0 dB below the bed. Duck does not apply that cut.

### Bed dips under speech

Lowpass at 200 Hz isolates the bed.
The 1.2 s to 1.8 s bed window measures -38.2 dB mean.
The same window in the mix measures -43.5 dB mean. That is a 5.3 dB dip.

Decoded `mix - speech` versus the bed is -5.316684 dB in that window.
Overlay on the same files stays at -38.2 dB. The dip exists only on the Duck path.

### Dubbed speech keeps full volume

Highpass at 800 Hz isolates the voice.
Speech and mix both measure -32.2 dB mean in the 1.2 s to 1.8 s window.
Peaks measure -28.4 dB and -28.3 dB.

The same graph without `normalize=0` measures -38.2 dB mean in that voice band.
Default `amix` also measures -38.2 dB. Both are a 6.0 dB cut.
`normalize=0` is present and effective.
