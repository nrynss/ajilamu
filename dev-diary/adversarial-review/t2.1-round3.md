## What the Downstream Loop Actually Requires

T2.7 orchestrates segment, translate, synthesize, measure, and repair. Its board entry replays fixtures offline for determinism. It flags segment 8 and accounts for every expense. It never asks live output to equal the fixture.

Live segmentation evidence therefore needs four things. Validated segments with non-empty text, speaker, and emotion. A speaker set that covers the fixture voices with no unknowns, so T2.3 can assign distinct voices. Plausible timing that fits inside the clip and orders cleanly into slots. One itemized charge per invocation with correct units.

Exact count and per-id timing equality are not on that list. The fixture captures one model sample from 2026-09-07. Later runs legitimately return 10 or 11 segments with shifted boundaries. My direction preserves every real signal (validation gate, speaker coverage, drift table, charge) while dropping the exactness requirement that valid runs cannot meet.
