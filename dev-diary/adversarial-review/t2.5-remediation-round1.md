## The Owner Decision, Implemented

**Short 0.05, long 0.08, named as defaults.** `DefaultMaxStretchShort` and
`DefaultMaxStretchLong` replace the two constants that both read `types.FitThreshold`. The doc
comment on the short side says the number comes from general speech time tailoring practice and
that it is UNVERIFIED BY EAR. It points at the listening ladder as the instrument that settles
it.

**The budget is an input.** `StretchLimits` carries a resolved short and long pair.
`PlanStretchWithLimits` and `StretchWithLimits` take it. `PlanStretch` and `Stretch` call them
with `DefaultStretchLimits()`. The package resolves nothing and knows nothing about language or
content slice. The doc says the caller layers project default, then language, then content
slice, narrowest wins, and that T2.6 or T2.7 owns that.

**I did not touch `types.FitThreshold`.** `stretch.go` no longer reads it at all. A comment
block states the divergence plainly. `Fits()` asks whether a take ships as it stands, and the
stretch budget asks whether a take repairs locally. `TestCallerSuppliedLimitsDriveTheRouting`
pins a take that reports `Fits` true at 6 percent short and still plans a rewrite.
