## The Package Scope Boundary and Sibling Files

Finding 1 identified the package scope boundary. Moving `stretchRatio`, `renderCandidate`, and `prepareRatio` into `stretch_test.go` prevents non-test sibling files from compiling against them.

Go package scope cannot prevent sibling files in `package fit` from calling unexported functions like `applyStretch` or `renderStretch`. Production files like `internal/fit/rewrite.go` (T2.6) and `internal/fit/loop.go` (T2.7) will compile in `package fit`.

Only a clear written rule defends against sibling abuse. `stretch.go` now documents this reality on `applyStretch` and `renderStretch`. The T2.5 handoff entry in `dev-diary/PHASE-2-fit-loop.md` states the rule in "The bypass" section and in the "Next agent" note. Authors of T2.6 and T2.7 must not call `applyStretch` or `renderStretch` directly. They must route all repairs through `Stretch` or `StretchWithLimits`.
