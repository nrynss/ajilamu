## Charge split and total

The split cannot double count. `selectWorkspaceTakeCharges` reads `kind IN ('translate',
'synthesize')`. `selectWholePassCharges` reads `kind = 'segment'`. The two sets are disjoint.

The total matches the sum. `selectRunningTotal` sums every charge. The measured payload carried
one synthesize charge of 900000, one translate charge of 24000, and one whole-pass segment
charge of 4000. The total read 928000. The Details tab rendered `$0.000928` and the covers
sentence `1 segment call, 1 translation call, and 1 render call.` The sentence matches the
three counts.

No array marshalled null. The empty stored project returned `"segments":[]`, `"languages":[]`,
`"charges":[]`, and `"commits":[]`. The real project carried `"charges":[]` on the charge-less
take. The only `null` in either body was the whole-pass `"segment_id":null`, which the wire
requires.
