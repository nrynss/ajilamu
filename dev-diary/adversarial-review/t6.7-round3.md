## 1. The run commit raced against edits

The harness seeded a fresh dub with a root commit and two lines. It started one real
`runRecorder.Persist` and the edit posts together on one barrier. It read the ledger with
outside SQL.

| Shape | Trials | Forks | version_seq | Head ancestry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One edit against one run commit | 20 | 0 | `1:1, 2:1, 3:1` in 20 of 20 | 3 in 20 of 20 |
| Twelve edits against one run commit | 20 | 0 | `1:1` through `14:1` in 20 of 20 | 14 in 20 of 20 |
| Twenty-four edits against one run commit | 6 | 0 | `1:1` through `26:1` in 6 of 6 | 26 in 6 of 6 |
| One edit, commit insert held 50 ms | 6 | 0 | `1:1, 2:1, 3:1` in 6 of 6 | 3 in 6 of 6 |

Every edit answered 201. The edit commit and the run commit both sat on the head chain in every
trial. Twelve edits put twelve edit commits on the head chain. Twenty-four edits put twenty-four
on it.

The control removed `api.LockDubCommit` from `runRecorder.persist` in a `/tmp` copy. One edit
against one run commit forked in 5 of 20 trials. Every fork held `version_seq` 2 twice, a head
ancestry of 2 and zero edits on the head chain. The same shape with the commit insert held 50 ms
forked in 6 of 6 trials. The harness detects the defect.

## 3. The re-render path shares the lock

`RerenderHandler` calls `recorder.Persist` on the target-text path and `PersistCorrected` on the
corrected-source path. Both reach `runRecorder.persist`, which takes `api.LockDubCommit`.

A throwaway probe held `api.LockDubCommit(dubID)`, posted both bodies to the real route and
counted `commits_raw` inserts at the store. Both bodies appended zero commits while the lock was
held. After the release both answered 201 and appended one commit. The probe failed on the
persist mutant with one insert while the lock was held.
