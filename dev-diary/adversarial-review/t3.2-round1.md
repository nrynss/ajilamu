### Crossfade policy (F1)

The earlier body from 0.2 s to 0.8 s matches the prepared take at -21.1 dB.
Those decoded windows share SHA-1 `7329bb4f78a81d5d3dfe1d8c1e252d471ce6521e`.
The later take body from 1.2 s to 1.6 s also measures -21.1 dB.
The later take therefore starts after the cut and does not overwrite the earlier body.

The last 40 ms fade down and the first 40 ms fade up.
They meet at 1.000 s with no overlapped samples.
`silencedetect=noise=-40dB:d=0.005` reports 7.4 ms of silence across that junction.
A decoded stereo frame at t=1.000000 is exactly 0.

This violates the rule that colliding speech must crossfade or truncate cleanly.
It also cuts voiced audio with a silent hole at the boundary.
