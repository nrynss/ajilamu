## Why I Did Not Bound The Error Text

The reviewer left the choice to me and I chose the caution alone. Three reasons.

ffmpeg puts its reason last. The 17 line message opens with the version line and the full
`configuration:` line, and reaches `Error opening output file` near the end. A head trim keeps
the banner and drops the reason, which makes the error worse rather than shorter.

A tail trim needs a policy about how much tail, and it would silently cut the path and the
argument list a reader needs. That is a formatting decision about ffmpeg output, which lives in
`internal/media` where the string gets built. T0.3 owns it and the reviewer upheld that call.

Bounding well inside this seam needs a new exported helper so a caller can get a short form.
Adding exported API nobody reviewed is outside a remediator's job. The caution costs a caller
nothing and the sentinel already carries every classification a caller needs.

## Contract Notes

These sit outside the T2.5 seam. I record each and acted on none.

**1. `types.Fit.Delta` is a second name for a derivable value, and P1 owns the question.**
`types.Fit` exports `Slot`, `Measured` and `Delta`, and the third is `Measured - Slot`. A
composite literal may omit it, and Go fills it with zero. A literal may also contradict it, and
nothing in the type notices. `Fits()`, `TooLong()` and `TooShort()` all read the field, so
`types.Fit{Slot: 7110ms, Measured: 4200ms}` still reports `Fits` true and `TooShort` false for a
take running 40.9 percent short. `internal/fit` no longer follows it, and my new test pins that
disagreement so nobody mistakes it for a bug in this package. Three consumers now re-derive the
invariant defensively. `internal/ledger/takes.go`, `internal/fixtures/load.go` and
`internal/fit/stretch.go`. Making `Delta` a method rather than a field would settle it for every
consumer at once, and dropping the field would break any caller that sets it. That decision is
P1's. `internal/types/types.go` is frozen and I did not touch it.

**2. The ceiling binds per call, and T2.6 or T2.7 must cap the total.** Six legal calls reached a
40.9 percent total correction. `internal/fit` holds no provenance for a take and cannot detect
that its input was its own earlier output, so nothing inside this seam can fix it. The loop must
carry the running total per take and must refuse a source that is already a stretched output.
The handoff's "Next agent" note now says both.

**3. `media.Run` and `media.Duration` build unbounded error strings, and T0.3 owns it.** One
