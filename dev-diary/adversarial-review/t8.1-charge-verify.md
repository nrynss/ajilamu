## Why the charges are missing

The measurement is direct. The mechanism below is a static reading of the deployed code.

The pipeline wraps its charge recorder in `trackingRecorder`. That wrapper appends to its own `recorded` slice only when a client calls its `Add`. The fix reads that slice through `RecordedCharges()`. So the ledger receives rows only when `Add` reaches the wrapper.

In production the wrapper never receives a call. `cmd/ajilamu/main.go` builds the Gemini clients with a `chargeRouter` and the synthesizer with a `cost.Ledger`. Neither `gemini.vertexTranslator` nor the Chirp synthesizer implements `SetRecorder`. Main.go does not wrap them in `fit.TranslatorProxy` or `fit.SynthesizerProxy`. The pipeline's `SetRecorder(tracker)` type assertions therefore fail. The clients keep the router and the ledger, and the wrapper's `recorded` slice stays empty. `tracker.Total()` and `tracker.Charges()` still delegate to the inner ledger. That is why the run reported the full cost while `RecordedCharges()` was empty.
