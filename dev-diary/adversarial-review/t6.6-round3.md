## What requireSelectOnlyGrants accepts and rejects

`requireSelectOnlyGrants` reads every `GRANT <privileges> ON` statement in the text. It
accepts the text only when every comma-separated entry between GRANT and ON equals
SELECT after trimming and uppercasing. It needs at least one such statement.

Accept. A SELECT-only grant set. Several SELECT rows. A grant that adds
`WITH GRANT OPTION`. A row with no ON is invisible to the parser, so a role row does not
fail the check.

Reject. An empty string, and any text with no `GRANT ... ON`. Any entry that is not
exactly SELECT, known or unknown, upper or lower case. A column-scoped `SELECT(col, col)`
fails closed, because the comma split breaks the parenthesised list.
