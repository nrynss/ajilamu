### Observations outside the findings

The new HTTP route and the two adapters carry no committed test. T7.4a owns no `internal/api` or
`cmd` test path, so the pin needs a contract extension. The live sweep in step 4 is the only
measurement of the supplied route today. The store itself is well pinned.

`OpenSettings` chmods a pre-existing directory on every open. A read-only data directory
therefore stops startup with a chmod error. The specification does not address that case.
