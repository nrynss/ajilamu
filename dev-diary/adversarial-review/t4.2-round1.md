## Scope and evidence

I reviewed the uncommitted `internal/ledger/takes.go` implementation against T4.2,
T1.1, T1.2, T1.3, and the landed T4.1 queue contract.

An isolated local HTTP capture harness exercised `Client.RecordTake` through the actual
T4.1 durable transport. It replayed ten fixture-shaped takes, with both stretched takes,
and one translate plus one synthesize charge per take.

The capture contained ten `takes_raw` JSONEachRow records and thirty `charges_raw` records.
Gemini usage produced separate `prompt_tokens` and `candidate_tokens` rows. Synthesis produced
one `characters` row. All row identities repeated the take attempt identity.

The segment 8 take carried `slot_ms: 7110`, `measured_ms: 4200`, and `delta_ms: -2910`.
An inconsistent delta was rejected before the capture received a request. A retry emitted the
same natural take identity into the raw tables. T1.3's server-owned event keys collapse that
replay through the `takes` and `charges` views.

`go test ./...`, `go vet ./...`, `gofmt -d internal/ledger/takes.go`, and
`git diff --check -- internal/ledger/takes.go` passed. The harness was outside the repository.
It did not alter an owned source or test path.
